Where does traffic get stuck?

Roads with the most combined waiting time come first. Select a road to find it on the map.

How to read this list

Queued means cars on the road now. Routed means cars whose remaining route uses it. Both fall to zero when a run ends. Delay keeps the record of the jam.

When a queue backs into another street, delay is assigned to the road at the front of the jam. One vehicle-hour is 60 car-minutes of waiting.

Which towns need attention?

Select a town to zoom in. Select a column heading to sort.

What the columns mean

Cars: participating flood-zone vehicles. Out: share that reached an exit. Last: last successful arrival, even if others were stranded. No route: cars unable to get out. No car: households needing other transport, not simulated here.
